Welcome to my website! I am a PhD student in Human Development and Family Science at Syracuse University and a graduate student associate at the Aging Studies Institute (ASI). I earned both my Master’s and Bachelor’s degrees from Seoul National University in South Korea. Mostly using quantitative statistical analyses via STATA, my research focuses on grandparenting, family demography, and family policy in both U.S. and South Korean contexts.
